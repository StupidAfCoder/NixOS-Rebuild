The design contract

01 / Pixel, not noisy

Small stepped cuts, square icons and a 4px spacing grid. No glass blur, repeated screws, glow, scanlines or bouncy scaling. Pixel headings; readable Cozette body text in the real shell.

02 / One color, many moods

One seed selected from the wallpaper. Choose true black, neutral, tonal, expressive, paper, or pure monochrome. More variety across themes, not a rainbow inside every card.

03 / Adapts, not shrinks

Cards clamp to the usable screen. Grids reflow, long lists scroll, and headers/actions stay reachable. Popovers open beside their rail control and flip at screen edges.
